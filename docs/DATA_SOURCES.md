## Auxiliary Hardware Sources

1. **Battery Management Systems (BMS)**:
   - Parametric costs, sensing channels, and cutoff ratings derived from commercial 48V smart BMS modules (TI BQ76952 family, Orion BMS 2 specifications).
2. **Thermal Cooling Hardware**:
   - Natural air convection ($10\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$) and forced air ($35\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$) derived from empirical cooling correlation literature (Incropera & DeWitt, *Fundamentals of Heat and Mass Transfer*).
3. **Reference Urban Drive Cycle**:
   - Synthetic 30-minute 1 Hz power profile deterministically generated by `scripts/gen_drive_cycle.py` (seed=42) representing typical urban commuter duty with 6 kW peak acceleration bursts.
